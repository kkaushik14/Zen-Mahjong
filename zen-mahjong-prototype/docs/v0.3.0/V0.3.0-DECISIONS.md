# v0.3.0 Confirmed Decisions

Only confirmed product direction belongs in this document. Deferred details are tracked in `V0.3.0-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.

## V3-DEC-001: Delivery scope

- Build an offline-complete mobile game before implementing backend services.
- Preserve ports for future account, synchronization, remote configuration, and advertisement providers.
- Do not make ordinary offline gameplay depend on those providers.

## V3-DEC-002: Versioned documentation

- Documents from earlier versions remain unchanged.
- `v0.3.0` decisions and specifications live under `docs/v0.3.0/`.
- Each later release receives a new version directory.

## V3-DEC-003: Tier structure

- One tier contains eight distinct, incrementally numbered levels.
- Levels 1 and 2 in a tier are Normal Concentration.
- Levels 3 and 4 are High Concentration.
- Levels 5 and 6 are Super Concentration.
- Levels 7 and 8 are Special Concentration.
- The next tier continues with levels 9 through 16 and repeats the four-category structure.

## V3-DEC-004: Initial assist inventory

On the first installation, the active local user receives:

| Assist  | Quantity |
| ------- | -------: |
| Shuffle |        2 |
| Undo    |        4 |
| Hint    |        6 |

Signing in later must not reset or duplicate previously consumed inventory.

## V3-DEC-005: Concentration Tray

- Four ordered regular slots receive eligible tiles.
- Matching regular-slot tiles resolve automatically.
- A separate special slot can resolve a tray tile with its board counterpart when a use is available.
- Four unmatched regular tiles with no available recovery produce a failed board state.
- Normal Concentration includes two special-slot uses, High includes one, Super includes zero, and Special Concentration includes two.

## V3-DEC-006: Revival pricing

- Concentration Score revival prices form the sequence `10, 15, 25, 40, 50, 65, 75, 90...` within one game.
- The first base increment is `10`; the second base increment is `15`.
- Each later first price is the last quoted price plus `10`.
- Each later second price is the last quoted price plus `15`.

## V3-DEC-007: Progression and replay

- Levels advance in incremental order.
- Completed levels are not replayable in the planned player flow.
- A completed eight-level tier produces one Concentration Score calculation.

## V3-DEC-008: Streak

- The first qualifying visit is streak day `1`.
- A visit on the next device-calendar day continues the streak.
- Missing a device-calendar day breaks the streak.
- The next qualifying visit starts again at day `1`.
- Device date and time-zone boundaries define the local streak calendar until account authority is implemented.

## V3-DEC-009: Profile and offline direction

- Only one profile is active at a time.
- A verified user may log out and log in with another identity later.
- Cached profile information remains visible offline.
- Offline gameplay remains functional; account-only actions report that connectivity is required.
- A future manual Sync action will submit validated offline events when connectivity returns.

## V3-DEC-010: Tutorial scope

- First launch presents a guided tutorial board.
- The tutorial explains board availability, the regular tray, and the special tray.
- It does not teach scoring formulas or the broader economy.
- A supported practice session follows before level 1.

## V3-DEC-011: Achievement delivery

- Achievement rewards are granted automatically.
- A specific achievement reward is granted once.
- Local receipts prevent duplicate grants after reload or retry.

## V3-DEC-012: Supported board and device range

- Board formats include 36, 72, and 144 tiles.
- Initial release validation prioritizes 36 and 72 tiles while retaining 144-tile capacity.
- Minimum practical width is 360 points on iOS and 360 dp on Android.
- Target devices are iPhone 11 or newer on iOS 16+, and 64-bit Android 10+ devices with at least 4 GB RAM.
- Gameplay targets 60 FPS with Reduced Motion available.

## V3-DEC-013: Quotes

- Reviewed quotes may appear sequentially on the loading screen.
- Quote content is data-driven and not hard-coded into screen components.
- Product ownership reviews attribution and cultural accuracy before publication.
