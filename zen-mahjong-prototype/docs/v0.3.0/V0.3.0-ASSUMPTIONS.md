# v0.3.0 Assumptions

These assumptions may guide implementation but are not confirmed product rules. Each must be validated or replaced by a later versioned decision.

## V3-ASM-001: Board geometry

The existing unit-footprint overlap model will be extended to support event blockers and larger boards. Exact side-contact tolerances, partial overlap, and event collision geometry require visual fixtures.

## V3-ASM-002: Required-assist boards

When a content contract requires an assist, the attempt records and protects the required capability so a restored session cannot become impossible because inventory changed after generation.

## V3-ASM-003: Offline reward authority

Offline rewards are recorded as versioned ledger transactions and completion receipts. A future server will validate and replay transactions instead of trusting a client-supplied final balance.

## V3-ASM-004: Offline level reconciliation

When level 23 is stored remotely and validated offline completion reaches level 55, successful reconciliation advances account progression to level 55 without replaying levels 24-55 in the UI. Duplicate completion receipts are ignored.

## V3-ASM-005: Offline balance reconciliation

The displayed post-sync balance is derived by applying validated offline earning and spending transactions to the last accepted revision. A raw local balance does not overwrite a server balance.

## V3-ASM-006: Tutorial economy isolation

Tutorial and practice sessions do not consume permanent assists, award Zen Score, advance streaks, grant achievements, or advance the numbered level sequence.

## V3-ASM-007: Achievement chaining

Concentration Score granted by an achievement may update the displayed balance but does not recursively trigger another score-threshold achievement in the same transaction. The next ordinary score transaction reevaluates eligibility.

## V3-ASM-008: Advertisement development provider

Development uses a deterministic fake advertisement provider. Production ad SDKs and fraud-resistant verification are deferred.

## V3-ASM-009: Local storage

MMKV stores versioned JSON-serializable documents, ledgers, receipts, and pending events. Future authentication secrets use platform-secure storage rather than MMKV or a loose JSON file.

## V3-ASM-010: Completion-time comparison

The faster-completion bonus compares with the immediately previous completed numbered level, even when the category changes. This assumption follows the current "last board" wording and needs explicit confirmation if category-normalized comparison is desired.

## V3-ASM-011: Special Concentration naming

`Special Concentration` is the `v0.3.0` name and replaces the earlier working term `Special Relaxation`.

## V3-ASM-012: Server retention

Server data recovery through day 90 and permanent deletion from day 91 are provisional and excluded from the offline implementation. The rule may change after privacy, legal, cost, and lifetime-retention review.
