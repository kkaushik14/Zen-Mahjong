# v0.3.0 Open Questions

The major offline gameplay direction is confirmed. The following items remain deferred or require approval before their related implementation segment is release-complete.

## Gameplay and economy

1. Does an assist-required board reserve the required assist at level creation, grant a board-scoped use, or only verify inventory before starting?
2. Does Restart consume inventory, score, or another cost?
3. Can achievement-awarded Concentration Score immediately unlock another score achievement, or is chaining prohibited as currently assumed?
4. How do achievement milestones continue after Concentration Score 200?
5. Should completion-time comparison use the immediately previous level or a comparable category and board size?

## Advertisements

6. Which advertisement placements are approved?
7. What cooldowns, daily limits, consent rules, and failure states apply?
8. What makes an advertisement result qualifying and how will production duplicate rewards be prevented?
9. Does an advertisement that grants a Shuffle, Undo, or Hint always add `+1` Zen Score?

## Tiles and presentation

10. What are the final special tile families, artwork, and event behavior?
11. Which Zen objects, environmental themes, sounds, and haptics are approved for completion?
12. What quote fields, attribution requirements, locales, and publication review states are required?
13. Have all Concentration frames been reviewed and exported from the production Figma file?

## Deferred online scope

14. Will account data eventually be retained for life or deleted after the provisional 90-day recovery period?
15. Which offline events are mergeable, which require user choice, and which can never be imported?
16. What production security and server validation are required before offline assist, score, advertisement, and purchase events can affect an account?

Backend framework, hosting, OAuth, MongoDB, remote configuration, and cloud operations are intentionally outside the current implementation plan.
