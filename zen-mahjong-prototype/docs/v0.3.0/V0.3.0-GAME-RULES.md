# v0.3.0 Game Rules

## Tile identity and matching

- Every tile instance has a unique instance ID.
- Every tile design has a stable design ID.
- Two tiles are the same for gameplay when their match identity is the same. Example: two Apple tiles match.
- `matching` describes two same tiles currently eligible to resolve.
- Similar tiles may depict the same broad subject with meaningful visual differences but must have different match identities. Example: a green-colored Orange design and an orange-colored Orange design do not match unless their configured match identity is the same.
- Special tiles are event-based or color-based content outside the regular catalogue. Their final visual families remain deferred.
- Visual decoys are excluded from this version until separately specified.

## Board availability

A board tile cannot enter the Concentration Tray when any of these conditions apply:

1. It is horizontally trapped between blocking tiles on both sides.
2. Its footprint is covered by a higher tile in a stack.
3. It is hidden or blocked by an active event object.
4. It has already been removed or already occupies the tray.

All other active board tiles are eligible. Exact collision footprints and event-object geometry must be represented by data and verified with fixtures before content production.

## Concentration Tray

- Selecting an eligible board tile moves it into the first available regular slot.
- A selected tile does not need to match an existing tray tile.
- Four regular slots preserve insertion order.
- When two entries share a match identity, they resolve automatically and leave the tray.
- Remaining entries compact while preserving their relative order.
- Four unmatched entries block further regular insertion.
- Tap is the accessible baseline interaction. Drag may provide an equivalent interaction.

## Special slot

- The special slot is separate from the four regular slots.
- Moving a tray tile into the special slot locates its matching eligible board tile and resolves the pair.
- Included uses per game are defined by difficulty:

| Category              | Included uses |
| --------------------- | ------------: |
| Normal Concentration  |             2 |
| High Concentration    |             1 |
| Super Concentration   |             0 |
| Special Concentration |             2 |

## Failure and recovery

The board does not use a generic random `no legal pair` failure. Published boards must have a validated solution contract.

A game fails when the regular tray contains four unmatched tiles and the player cannot or does not use an available recovery. Recovery choices may include:

- Restart the current level.
- Spend Concentration Score on revival.
- Spend Concentration Score on additional special-slot capacity or uses when that product rule is enabled.
- Use a reward advertisement when online and the provider is available.
- Use Zen Tokens after in-app purchase is introduced in a later scope.

Offline presentation must not display an unavailable advertisement as a usable action.

## Difficulty and solvability

- Normal boards are guaranteed solvable without requiring scarce assists.
- High boards increase visual similarity and solution complexity.
- Super boards further increase similarity and complexity.
- Special boards draw from the special-tile catalogue and may combine prior special designs.
- When offline inventory has no required assist, generated content must remain solvable without spending an assist.
- When the user has assists, higher difficulty may select a validated solution contract that requires an assist. Required-assist behavior must not consume an unavailable balance or create an unrecoverable restored session.
- Every generated board stores its seed, content version, difficulty contract, and validated solution evidence.

## Level sequence

Each tier has eight distinct levels:

| Tier offset | Category              |
| ----------: | --------------------- |
|         1-2 | Normal Concentration  |
|         3-4 | High Concentration    |
|         5-6 | Super Concentration   |
|         7-8 | Special Concentration |

Level numbering never resets. Tier 1 is levels 1-8, tier 2 is 9-16, and so on.

## Assist behavior

- Undo restores the complete previous accepted transaction, including board, tray, counters, costs, and local ledger effects that belong to that transaction.
- Hint identifies a deterministic valid action for the current state.
- Shuffle produces a deterministic, validated arrangement and must preserve the applicable solvability contract.
- Restart restores the level's initial content and starts a fresh attempt according to the confirmed cost policy.
- Assist limits belong to inventory and content configuration, not component-local counters.

## Tutorial and practice

- Tutorial interaction covers board availability, regular tray insertion, automatic matching, and the special tray.
- Tutorial and practice economy effects remain isolated from ordinary progression unless explicitly approved in the assumptions or open questions.
