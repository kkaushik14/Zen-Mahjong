# v0.3.0 Tile Artwork Production Brief

## Document status

- Version: `0.3.0`
- Purpose: artwork planning and design review for the initial product release
- Scope: 50 proposed tile themes
- Relationship to existing documents: this is a new artwork-production brief. It does not replace the tile catalogue specification or generated master sheet.
- Classification status: proposed for artwork production and subject to visual approval before catalogue status changes to `approved`

## Release allocation

| Role                | Count | Purpose                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ----: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Core regular tiles  |    36 | Main board content for Normal, High, and Super Concentration, and the regular portion of Special Concentration |
| Special tiles       |     8 | Exclusive pool for Special Concentration levels                                                                |
| Reserve/event tiles |     6 | Held for later events, themed rooms, or promotion into the regular catalogue after readability testing         |
| Total               |    50 | Initial release artwork scope                                                                                  |

The 36 regular identities support the three target board sizes cleanly:

| Board size | Recommended identities | Instances per identity |            Result |
| ---------: | ---------------------: | ---------------------: | ----------------: |
|   36 tiles |                      9 |                      4 | 18 matching pairs |
|   72 tiles |                     18 |                      4 | 36 matching pairs |
|  144 tiles |                     36 |                      4 | 72 matching pairs |

Not every level must use the maximum number of identities. Every placed identity must appear an even number of times. Four visually identical instances per identity are recommended because they create two matching pairs while preserving planning choices.

## Matching and visual rules

1. Two regular tiles match only when their stable `matchIdentity` values are equal.
2. Every instance with the same identity uses the same artwork. Do not rotate, mirror, recolor, crop, or decorate one matching instance differently.
3. Similar-looking tiles share a `similarityGroup` but retain different match identities and must not match.
4. Shape and marker must remain sufficient without color. Color is supporting information only.
5. The motif artwork must not contain selection, hint, availability, blocked, reward, or rarity states. The application UI supplies those states.
6. Do not draw the physical tile face, border, shadow, or bevel into the motif asset. The reusable tile component owns the tile material.
7. Special artwork may feel rarer, but it must use the same tile proportions and remain readable beside regular tiles.
8. Reserve/event artwork should still follow the same source and export standards so it can be promoted without redrawing.

## Difficulty usage

### Normal Concentration

- Prefer distinct silhouettes with low confusion risk.
- Introduce new visual families gradually.
- Do not place two members of the same similarity group on the earliest boards.
- Recommended first-board set: Bamboo, Crane, Garden Gate, Leaf, Lotus, Moon, Mountain, Sun, and Water.

### High Concentration

- Introduce a limited number of similarity groups.
- Use no more than two or three visually related groups on one early High board.
- Differences should remain discoverable through silhouette and marker, not only through color.

### Super Concentration

- Use more members from the same similarity groups.
- Preserve fair recognition at the minimum tile size.
- Difficulty may come from visual comparison, layering, and layout, but not from misleading match rules.

### Special Concentration

- Mix regular identities from the full eligible catalogue with four identities selected from the eight-tile Special pool.
- Special selection must be deterministic in game content even when the artwork pool is reused.
- Do not repeat the exact previous four-design Special set when another valid combination is available.
- Every selected Special identity must have an even instance count. Four instances per selected identity is the recommended default when board capacity permits.

## Artwork deliverables for every design

Each tile design requires the following deliverables:

1. **Source artwork**
   - Vector-first source in Figma, SVG, or another editable vector format.
   - Square artboard.
   - Motif centered optically, not only mathematically.
   - No baked tile background, shadow, border, or interaction state.

2. **Full motif asset**
   - Recommended review canvas: `512 x 512` pixels or equivalent vector view box.
   - Transparent background.
   - Uses no more than approximately 72% of the safe area unless the silhouette requires more space.

3. **Compact motif asset**
   - Simplified independently for board and tray rendering.
   - Must remain identifiable at `34 x 47`, `48 x 67`, and `64 x 89` tile sizes.
   - Remove decorative lines that disappear or merge at those sizes.

4. **Monochrome proof**
   - One-color rendering proving that the marker and silhouette work without color.

5. **Similarity proof**
   - Side-by-side comparison with every member of the same similarity group.
   - Include grayscale and color-blind simulation checks.

6. **Pair proof**
   - Four identical instances shown together.
   - Confirms that no instance-level variation could imply a different match identity.

7. **Metadata**
   - Stable design ID and match identity.
   - Display and accessibility names.
   - Family and similarity group.
   - Regular, Special, reserve, or event role.
   - Eligible concentration categories.
   - First intended introduction level.
   - Artwork version and approval status.

## Recommended asset naming

Use lowercase kebab-case derived from the stable design ID:

```text
tiles/<design-id>/source/<design-id>.svg
tiles/<design-id>/<design-id>-full.svg
tiles/<design-id>/<design-id>-compact.svg
tiles/<design-id>/review/<design-id>-monochrome.png
tiles/<design-id>/review/<design-id>-small-size.png
```

If raster exports become necessary, generate them from the approved vector source rather than editing raster copies independently.

## Palette guidance

- Use multiple natural color families rather than making every tile green.
- Suggested accents include jade, vermilion, indigo, mineral blue, moss green, muted gold, charcoal, and blossom pink.
- Give each identity one dominant color relationship, but never make color its only distinguishing property.
- Similar tiles may share a palette during High and Super Concentration, provided their markers remain distinct.
- Avoid high-saturation color on every tile at once; the full board must remain calm and scannable.
- Special tiles may receive a restrained shared accent, but the UI should own rarity glow, frame, and animation.

## Review sizes and conditions

Approve each design only after checking it in all of these conditions:

| Condition              | Requirement                                         |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Full board tile        | Clear at the largest supported tile presentation    |
| Minimum board tile     | Recognizable at approximately 34-pixel motif width  |
| Concentration tray     | Recognizable inside the compact tray tile           |
| 36-tile board          | Distinct with comfortable spacing                   |
| 72-tile board          | Distinct under increased density                    |
| 144-tile board         | Recognizable without relying on zoom                |
| Grayscale              | Match identity remains clear                        |
| Color-blind simulation | Similarity remains challenging but fair             |
| Reduced Motion         | No identity depends on animation                    |
| Dimmed/blocked state   | Marker remains identifiable when opacity is reduced |
| Hint/selected state    | UI outline does not obscure the required marker     |

## Approval checklist

A tile can move from `draft` to `approved` only when all checks pass:

- [ ] Stable design ID and match identity are confirmed.
- [ ] Full and compact artwork are complete.
- [ ] Silhouette is recognizable at minimum size.
- [ ] Color-independent marker is visible.
- [ ] Matching instances are visually identical.
- [ ] Similarity-group comparisons are fair and intentional.
- [ ] Accessibility label is approved.
- [ ] Cultural review is complete where relevant.
- [ ] Artwork does not contain UI-owned states or tile framing.
- [ ] 36-, 72-, and 144-tile board previews have been reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] iOS and Android rendering have both been inspected.
- [ ] Source and export filenames follow the catalogue ID.
- [ ] Content version and approval status are recorded.

## Assumptions requiring later confirmation

1. The 36/8/6 allocation is an artwork-production recommendation, not yet a change to the machine-readable catalogue.
2. Special identities are treated as exact-match identities for artwork purposes unless a later gameplay decision explicitly defines family matching.
3. The Special pool uses four selected identities per Special Concentration level.
4. Reserve/event tiles are not required for the first complete regular level sequence.
5. The Meditation Figure remains culturally neutral until the intended religious treatment is approved.
6. Final palette values, material texture, audio relationship, and environment-specific effects will be approved during visual production.
7. Level-specific identity rotation and exact instance counts remain deterministic game-content responsibilities, not artwork responsibilities.

## Production order

1. Finish and approve the original nine Normal Concentration tiles.
2. Produce the remaining distinct regular silhouettes.
3. Produce and compare every High/Super similarity family.
4. Produce the eight Special tiles and review them together as one pool.
5. Produce the six reserve/event tiles after the release-active set is stable.
6. Generate the updated machine-readable catalogue and master sheet only after artwork and metadata approval.
